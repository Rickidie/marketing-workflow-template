## Platform-Specific Guidance

### Blog posts
- Open with the problem or a sharp claim, not background context
- Use headings as signposts — a reader scanning headings should understand the argument
- Specific metrics and named examples beat vague claims
- End with a clear, direct CTA (load from `brand-facts.example.md`)

### LinkedIn
- Open with the point, not a wind-up
- Short paragraphs — 1 to 3 lines maximum
- No "I'm humbled to share..." or "Excited to announce..."
- CTA should direct to a next action, not prompt comments for engagement

### X (Twitter)
- Lead with the strongest claim or tension
- Specificity beats adjectives — metrics, named outcomes, exact numbers
- 280 characters max; threads must earn their length
- No engagement bait

## Editorial Standards

- Zero em-dashes in deliverables. Use commas, parentheses, or sentence breaks.
- Sentence-length variation — mix short punchy sentences with longer ones where the content earns it.
- Humanise tone: write how a knowledgeable colleague talks, not how a press release reads.
- Pair any autonomy claim with an auditability claim where relevant to your domain.
